RiseGrid

How to play RiseGrid

The rules, controls and scoring of RiseGrid for iPhone and iPad (version 1.3).

RiseGrid is a block puzzle turned upside down. Instead of falling, the pieces rise from the bottom of the board and stack up at the top. Fit them together, fill whole rows to clear them, and keep the stack from growing all the way down.

The basics

  • The board is 10 columns wide and 20 rows tall.
  • Each new piece enters at the bottom centre of the board and rises one row per second. The speed stays the same for the whole game.
  • A piece stops when it reaches the top of the board or touches a block above it. After a short moment (half a second) it locks in place and the next piece enters.
  • When a row is completely filled, it clears. Everything below the cleared row moves up to close the gap.
  • A faint outline, the ghost piece, shows where the active piece will stop.
  • The preview to the right of the board shows which piece comes next.

Controls

GestureWhat it does
Drag left or rightMoves the piece sideways, one column for each short step of your finger.
Tap the boardRotates the piece. You can tap anywhere on the board.
Swipe upLaunches the piece straight to where the ghost piece shows. It locks after the usual short moment.
Swipe down, or tap the HOLD slotPuts the piece on hold (see below).
Pause button (top right)Pauses the game and opens the menu with Settings.

A piece that has stopped can still be moved or rotated to slide it into place. Each move restarts the short locking moment, but only 15 times per piece: after that, the piece locks however fast you keep tapping.

The pieces

RiseGrid uses the seven classic four-block shapes (I, O, T, L, J, S and Z) plus two special pieces that turn up less often: a five-block plus (a cross) and a single block. The O, the plus and the single block don't rotate.

Hold piece

  • Swipe down, or tap the HOLD slot to the left of the board, to keep the current piece for later.
  • If the slot was empty, the next piece comes into play. If a piece was already held, the two swap. The piece that comes out starts again from the bottom.
  • You can hold once per piece: a piece that has just come out of the slot can't go straight back in. The slot looks dimmed while holding isn't allowed.
  • The held piece is kept when your game is saved.

Scoring

Every piece you place scores 10 points. Clearing several rows with one piece pays more than clearing them one at a time:

Rows cleared with one piecePoints
1 row100
2 rows300
3 rows500
4 rows800

The points for a clear pop up on the board. Your score, your best score and the play time are shown on screen while you play.

Game over

The game ends when the stack has grown so far down that a new piece has no room to enter at the bottom. The game-over screen shows your score and your place in the Top 10, or how many more points you needed to get in. Tap restart to play again.

RiseGrid keeps your best score, a Top 10 list and your most recent runs on your device.

Zen mode

Zen mode is a relaxed way to play. Turn it on in Pause → Settings → Zen mode.

  • There is no game over: when a new piece has no room, the top six rows clear quietly and everything below moves up.
  • There are no points. The score display shows “Zen mode” instead.
  • A game that used Zen mode at any point doesn't count towards your best score or the Top 10, even if you switch Zen mode off before it ends.

Modes and themes

The rules are the same in every mode; only the look changes. Pick a mode in Pause → Settings:

  • Classic: plain blocks.
  • Fun: animated themes, Glow, Balloons, Station and Stars, with optional pushers (flames, propeller, cars, a monster truck, a bulldozer, a fire engine and more) that lift the piece as it rises.

Tutorial

Your first game starts with a short guided tutorial that shows each gesture on the real board (move, rotate, launch and hold) and waits until you try it. You can skip it and replay it any time from Pause → Settings → How to play.

Saving your game

Your game in progress is saved on your device. Close the app and it picks up where you left off.
