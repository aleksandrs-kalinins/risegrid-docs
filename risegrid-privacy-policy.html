RiseGrid Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

RiseGrid is developed and published by Aleksandrs Kalinins.

I respect your privacy. RiseGrid is designed as an offline game and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or usage data.

Information I Collect

RiseGrid does not collect personal information or other user data.

The game does not require an account, registration, login, or submission of personal information.

RiseGrid does not use advertising services, analytics services, tracking technologies, or third-party services that collect information about users.

Data Stored on Your Device

RiseGrid stores the following information locally on your device so that the game can work as expected:

  • your best score, and the play time at which it was reached;
  • your game in progress (the blocks on the board, your current score, elapsed play time, and the upcoming pieces), so that you can resume where you left off.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to me or to any third party.

You can remove your locally stored RiseGrid data by deleting the RiseGrid application from your device.

Third-Party Services

RiseGrid does not share user information with third parties and does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

The game uses Apple's operating-system frameworks required to run the application on Apple devices.

Links to This Website

The game does not connect to the internet by itself. The pause screen contains a link to this privacy policy. If you choose to open it, the page is loaded in your web browser from a third-party web host, which may receive technical information such as your IP address under its own policies. RiseGrid does not send any data when you do this.

Children's Privacy

RiseGrid does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Because RiseGrid does not collect personal information, no personal information is held by the developer.

Data Retention and Deletion

I do not collect or retain personal information through RiseGrid.

The best score and any saved game stored locally on your device can be removed by deleting the RiseGrid application.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to RiseGrid or applicable requirements.

When this policy is updated, the effective date at the top of this page will be changed accordingly.
